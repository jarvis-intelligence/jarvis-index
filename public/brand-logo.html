Identity

An aperture, not a face.

J.A.R.V.I.S. was never a robot on screen — it was a presence that listened and answered. The mark takes that idea rather than the artwork: a solid core wrapped by three arc blades that never quite close.

The mark is original. It deliberately avoids the arc reactor, the Stark HUD, and every other protected element of the films.

The core is your agent
One solid, bright form at the centre. Everything else exists to serve it.
Three blades: know, remember, do
Unequal lengths, so the ring reads as focusing rather than idling — a system in the middle of paying attention.
The gaps stay open
The ring never closes. Local-first, inspectable, and not a sealed box around your work.

Construction

Everything sits on one circle.

All three blades share a single radius and stroke. Only their arc lengths differ, which is what keeps the mark from reading as a loading spinner.

Colour

Four treatments, no others.

The blades carry the accent and the core carries the ink. On a single-colour surface — an embroidery, a stamp, a partner's logo wall — collapse both to one value.

GitHub organisation

The avatar is the mark on ink, full bleed.

No rounded corners baked in and no wordmark — GitHub applies its own crop, and at 20 px in a repo list the letters would be mud. The blades run slightly heavier here than in the web mark so they survive that crop.
